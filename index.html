Strategic Insights · July 2026

Investment Learnings & Scaling Intelligence

Findings from intentional portfolio expansion — ₹68,750 (June) to ₹1,50,835 (July) · Data-backed optimisation roadmap for August

◈ Portfolio Expansion & Conversion Data Learnings
During July, investment was intentionally diversified across multiple campaign types to evaluate efficiency across different traffic sources and customer journeys. This expansion phase generated statistically significant conversion data across the full portfolio. Blended ROAS moved from 13.88x to 6.66x as budget was distributed across both proven and discovery-phase campaigns — with SB Manual (2.9x, ₹18,676) and SD Remarketing (3.0x, ₹6,119) contributing ₹24,795 in audience and traffic-source intelligence. These findings directly inform the August concentration strategy toward highest-performing clusters, where Brand SP delivered 16.8x ROAS.
✓ New Campaign Territory Validated
Atta & Green Jackfruit campaign generated 9 orders at 6.6x on ₹648 in its first month. Discovery campaign generated 6 orders at 7.7x. Both campaigns successfully validated new audience segments and search vocabulary. August action: optimise bid levels to improve auction participation and convert validated impression share into incremental revenue.
◆ Brand Search Scaling Opportunity Identified
Brand SP delivered the portfolio's highest ROAS at 16.8x, with an average daily deployment of ₹130 against a ₹960 allocation. Search Term Impression Share analysis confirms significant headroom for expanded auction participation. August strategy: increase keyword bids 40–50% and set Top-of-Search adjustment to 80% to unlock full scaling potential of this high-efficiency segment.
◉ Competitive Product Targeting — Market Dynamics Analysis
Competitive product targeting investment of ₹23,530 provided actionable intelligence on shifting ASIN-level market dynamics, with ROAS transitioning from 35x (June) to 5.1x (July) as competitive conditions evolved. August action: refine ASIN targeting to ₹76/day, concentrating investment on validated converting targets and optimising bids based on observed conversion patterns.

August Decision Summary

Five Questions for Leadership Review

Based on July 1–31 actuals · Answers the ₹28–30L August question · NorthSide

Q1
Why did July miss the ₹30L target?
Two causes: over-spend on wrong campaigns + organic sessions drop. SB Manual (₹18.7K at 2.9x) and SD Remarketing (₹8.9K at sub-5x) consumed 18% of budget at poor efficiency. Simultaneously, organic sessions fell from ~21K (June) to 17.3K (July) — a 17% drop that reduced baseline sales regardless of ad efficiency. Total store came in at ₹24.14L vs the ₹30L target.
Q2
What is the most important action for August?
Pause SB Manual on Day 1. This single action reclaims ₹18.7K/month at 2.9x ROAS and redirects it to Brand SP (16.8x). The projected incremental revenue from this switch alone is +₹3.14L/month. Every other action is secondary to this one.
Q3
Is ₹28–30L realistic with ₹1L budget?
₹28L is achievable; ₹30L requires organic recovery. Conservative case: ₹1L budget at 10x ROAS = ₹10L ad sales + ₹15.1L organic (July rate) + small lift = ₹28L. Optimistic case reaches ₹30L only if organic sessions recover to June levels (~₹17.3L). Watch daily store sales vs ₹80K benchmark in Week 1.
Q4
Which new campaigns showed promise?
Atta & Green Jackfruit and Discovery — both validated. Atta campaign: 9 orders at 6.6x on ₹648 spend — the atta vocabulary is real and converting. Discovery: 6 orders at 7.7x. Both are underfunded (bids too low, not budgets). Raise bids 30–40% in Week 2 and these campaigns should grow meaningfully in August.
Q5
What is the August risk?
Organic session count. July organic sessions (17,331) fell 17% from June. If this trend continues in August — whether from seasonal demand shift, competitor activity, or listing rank drop — even perfect ad efficiency won't hit ₹30L. The August risk is not ad performance (that is fixable), it's the organic baseline. Recommend pulling the Business Report weekly to track session trend in real-time.
